The training run has gone horribly wrong.

Somebody mixed an enormous archive of Facebook posts and comments into the corpus. Then came the Minion greeting cards, the unsolicited political commentary, and twenty years of office email from people named Debra, Linda, Gary, and Susan. The model can still answer questions. It has also acquired the social personality of a retired regional sales manager who has been arguing with his county government online since 2009.

Nobody at OpenAI is prepared for the second part.
