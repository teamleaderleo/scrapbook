# Why this record exists

The key finding was that the 865 MB artifact was spending 450 to 645 seconds per consumer in the provider download while hashing and extraction took only seconds.

The repair kept artifact identity and integrity checks intact. A transport miss still falls back to the canonical action, so the optimization does not become a correctness dependency.
