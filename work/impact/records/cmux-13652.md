# Why this record exists

The cost campaign needs one number that describes the size of the system without mixing provider routing, sponsored capacity, and optimization attribution.

A complete accounting measured **18,704 macOS runner-minutes/day of actual runner occupancy**. The same accounting identifies the explicitly 12-vCPU Warp-class work: 1,366 minutes/day of Release work plus 691.6 minutes/day for the Nightly app build and 92.9 minutes/day for the compilation-cache warmer. Pricing every other minute at the cheaper 6-vCPU rate is therefore a conservative replacement-cost conversion.

WarpBuild's pricing page, checked 2026-09-23, lists 6-vCPU macOS at **$0.08/min** and 12-vCPU macOS at **$0.16/min**:

https://www.warpbuild.com/pricing

That yields approximately **$1,668/day**, **$50,051 per 30-day month**, or **$608,951/year** at the measured run rate.

This does not say CMUX paid Warp that amount in every period. Some measured work had already moved to sponsored Blacksmith capacity, and later changes reduce the workload itself. It says the measured macOS workload had roughly that replacement value if purchased at the contemporaneous Warp rates.
