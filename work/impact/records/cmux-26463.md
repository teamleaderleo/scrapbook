# Why this record exists

A lifecycle regression hibernates the harness while its task keeps producing output; a new harness reads from the prior output offset and stops the task. Generation checks and caller identity keep task ownership attached to the chat.

[PR 26447](https://redirect.github.com/manaflow-ai/cmux/pull/26447) exposes the tools and routes eligible Claude background shells to them, retaining a fallback when the replacement isn't available. [PR 30197](https://redirect.github.com/manaflow-ai/cmux/pull/30197) repairs a later restart race: a reconnect could arrive before the new daemon adopted the old caller token. Binding now waits for adoption and applies the same identity checks.

The [restart test receipt](https://redirect.github.com/manaflow-ai/cmux/pull/30197#issuecomment-6554426715) records red before the fix and green afterward, including wrong-chat and invalid-token refusals. These are stages of one lifecycle story, not separate feature totals.
