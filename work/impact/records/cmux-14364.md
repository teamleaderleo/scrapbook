# Why this record exists

The remaining cost after cmux#14349 looked like compile time but was module emission for a module no target imports. In the same run, lazy type checking left the emit-module job at 26 s and switching drivers alone kept it at 36 s; skipping the module entirely is what removed it. The change is per target, and the enumerated test inventory confirms nothing was dropped from the bundle.

This record shares the `cmux-cmuxtests-rebuild` overlap group with cmux#14349 because its before number (about 31 s) is #14349's after number on the same rebuild. Chained, the one-test-file cmuxTests rebuild went from 139 s to about 4 s across the two canaries, but they ran on different runners and commits, so the chain is not claimed as one number.
