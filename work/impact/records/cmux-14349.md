# Why this record exists

The cache looked like a pure win, yet for cmuxTests it disabled incremental builds outright: the driver remarks pinned every invalidation on the regenerated chained bridging header, which cmuxTests reaches through `@testable import` of the app. The fix is per target, so nothing else lost the cache.

cmux#14359 later applied the same finding to the app target on the macOS 15 pool (Xcode 26.3) and cites this change. cmux#14364 builds on this one by dropping cmuxTests' module emission; the two share the `cmux-cmuxtests-rebuild` overlap group because #14364's before number is this PR's after number.

An attempt to measure the win in real PR admissions before and after the merge found only three PRs touching nothing but cmuxTests among the CI runs from 00:50Z to 04:49Z on 2026-09-25, too few to compare, so the claims stay observed.
