# Why this record exists

The hold applies to the chat's manager even when the pair guard is otherwise disabled. The person's prompts still run, unrelated agents retain their prior behavior, and the manager can't remove its own hold.

Integration tests check unauthorized takeover, manager identity, held delivery and ordered handback. The source explicitly leaves wiring the banner to the new method for a follow-up. The merged backend mechanism is stronger evidence than a banner, but it isn't evidence of completed UI integration or stable release.
