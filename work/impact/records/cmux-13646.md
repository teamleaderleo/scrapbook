# Why this record exists

Most publication work in the sample was short-lived: 70 of 101 publishes were replaced by another publish within an hour, while the median publish pipeline itself took about 43 minutes.

The throttle deliberately accepts more delivery latency to free a scarce macOS pool. Schedules, release-candidate branches, manual dispatches, and failure cases keep their previous behavior.
