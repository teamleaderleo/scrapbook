# Why this record exists

The 12 s grace is deliberate product behavior (it leaves room for a SessionEnd hook); the tests paid it only because they closed surfaces while login(1) was still starting. The fix stays in tearDown and keeps the surfaces exec-mode, so the startup and sizing paths the suite tests are unchanged.
