# Why this record exists

The fix is small, but the cause was not visible in any build setting: it came from reading Swift Build's source after checksum-only mode left the re-emits in place. It closes the "every package re-emits on an unchanged tree" complaint for runners that have a seed of their own width. The remaining gaps are named rather than folded into the claim.
