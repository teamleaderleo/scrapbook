## Author's diagnosis

The first version stacked too many metaphors: launch ceremony, theatre, copy desk, institutional record, and magazine publication. Several paragraphs ended by repeating the previous point at a higher emotional volume.

The most obvious habits were:

- `The surprise comes from ...`
- `This is where ...`
- `The mistake is ...`
- `The answer is ...`
- `The copy desk is where ...`
- abstract section headings that promised more significance than the section earned.
